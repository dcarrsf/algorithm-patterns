# Practice Questions: Sliding Window

Types of questions that prompt using each example:

1. **Max Sum Subarray**: "Find the maximum sum of a subarray of size K" or "Calculate the highest sum of K consecutive elements in an array."

2. **Longest Substring**: "Find the longest substring with at most K distinct characters" or "Determine the length of the longest substring containing no more than K unique characters."

3. **Smallest Contiguous Array**: "Find the length of the smallest contiguous subarray whose sum is greater than or equal to a given value" or "Determine the shortest subarray that sums to at least S."

4. **Find Anagrams**: "Find all anagrams of a pattern in a string" or "Locate all occurrences of permutations of a given pattern in a string."
